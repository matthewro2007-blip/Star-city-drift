# Streets of Roanoke

Streets of Roanoke (formerly Star City Drift) is a finished 2D arcade beat 'em up set in Roanoke, Virginia, the Star City. You play as Matthew Rose, free-roaming the streets, clearing thug waves, and finishing the job. It is a static site of vanilla ES modules and canvas (no build step) and can be installed as an app on PC and phone.

Version 1.5.0 adds a free prequel DLC where you play as Silas Boone. Version 1.6.0 rewrites the story as "The Star Goes Dark" and adds the Dark Star Suit. Version 1.7.0, "Locked Up", added the arrest ending, the Cinder Hollow prison to explore after the story, the Cinder Hollow Orange outfit, New Game+, a Sunny / Night / Rainy weather switch, and the new name. Version 1.8.0 is the HD Remaster: every character and background redrawn at twice the detail, drawn at 1920x1080 on big screens. Version 1.9.0, "Cutscenes & Voices", adds real cutscenes, voiced dialogue with talking portraits, a 1x / 2x / 3x camera zoom, the 2015 prequel rework and Marcus Hale's side missions. Version 2.0.0 adds the free "Blackriver" DLC, set in 2028: Silas Boone breaks out of Cinder Hollow and goes after the Blackriver outfit, in a new story with its own cutscenes, voices, bosses and a red suit. Version 2.0.1 fixes the "two Silases" glitch and gives the prison new guards (C.O.s, riot and baton guards, a sergeant), with new looks and lines for Tumbler Voss and the Lockkeeper. Version 2.0.2 adds William Burks, Matthew's oldest friend, with four voiced side quests. Version 2.1.0 adds online co-op: one player is Matthew, a friend joins as William from an invite link or room code, on any device. It also brings all-new fight animations and redrawn characters across the cast.

Play it at https://matthewro2007-blip.github.io/Star-city-drift/

## Install as an app

- **Chrome or Edge on PC:** use the install icon in the address bar.
- **Android Chrome:** Add to Home screen, or Install app.
- **iPhone Safari:** Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## iPhone

The easiest way is Safari: open the game, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. It runs full screen, works offline and updates itself. Full steps are in [IPHONE.txt](downloads/iphone/IPHONE.txt).

There is also an unsigned iPhone app, [Streets-of-Roanoke-2.1.0.ipa](downloads/iphone/Streets-of-Roanoke-2.1.0.ipa), built by GitHub Actions. It isn't on the App Store. You sign it yourself with your own Apple ID using [AltStore](https://altstore.io) or [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io). With a free Apple ID it has to be re-signed every 7 days. A paid Apple developer account makes it last a year and allows TestFlight.

## Android APK

Download [Star-City-Drift.apk](downloads/Star-City-Drift.apk) (Streets of Roanoke v2.1.0) and sideload it. Allow installation from unknown sources when Android asks. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your saves. The app auto-updates when online.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrows move, Z punch, X kick, C heavy, E talk, Q camera zoom (1x / 2x / 3x), T weather (Sunny / Night / Rainy), 1–5 outfits, Esc pause/save. Weather is also on the title screen and in the pause menu, and it is remembered on this device.
- **Controllers:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox controllers are supported. Create / View cycles the weather.
- **Phones:** on-screen touch controls, plus a weather button.
