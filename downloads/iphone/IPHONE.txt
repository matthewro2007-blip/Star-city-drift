STREETS OF ROANOKE 1.7.0 ON AN IPHONE
=====================================

There are two ways to play on an iPhone or iPad. Option A is the easiest and is what
we recommend.

OPTION A (EASIEST, RECOMMENDED): ADD IT TO YOUR HOME SCREEN FROM SAFARI
  1. Open Safari and go to  https://matthewro2007-blip.github.io/Star-city-drift/
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap Add.
  Streets of Roanoke now opens full screen from its own icon, like any app. It works
  offline after the first load, updates itself when you're online and keeps your saves.
  No Apple ID, no computer, nothing to re-sign. Works on every iPhone and iPad.

OPTION B: SIDELOAD THE .IPA (FOR PEOPLE WHO WANT A "REAL" APP)
  Streets-of-Roanoke-1.7.0.ipa is NOT signed and is not on the App Store. You sign it
  yourself with your own Apple ID, using a free tool on a Mac or Windows PC:
    - AltStore   https://altstore.io        (installs AltServer on your computer)
    - Sideloadly https://sideloadly.io      (drag the .ipa in, enter your Apple ID)
  1. Download Streets-of-Roanoke-1.7.0.ipa.
  2. Connect your iPhone to the computer with a cable and trust the computer.
  3. In AltStore or Sideloadly, pick the .ipa and sign in with your Apple ID.
  4. On the iPhone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > your Apple ID > Trust.
     On iOS 16 and later also turn on Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode.
  5. Open Streets of Roanoke from the Home Screen.
  WITH A FREE APPLE ID the app stops opening after 7 days. Re-sign it (AltStore can
  refresh it for you over Wi-Fi) every 7 days. A free Apple ID can have 3 sideloaded
  apps at a time. Your saves stay on the phone when you re-sign.
  The .ipa plays the copy of the game built into it. For a newer game version, download
  the newer .ipa and sideload it over the old one (your saves are kept). Option A always
  has the latest version.

PAID APPLE DEVELOPER ACCOUNT ($99/year)
  If you have one, sign the .ipa with it instead and the app lasts a year before it
  needs re-signing. With a paid account the game could also be sent to testers through
  TestFlight (that needs a signed build uploaded to App Store Connect, which this
  unsigned .ipa is not).

WHICH ONE?
  Most people: Option A. It's the same game, it's free and it never expires.
