FIRST RUN
  The app isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC".
  Click "More info" > "Run anyway".
  F11 or Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen. Esc pauses. Saves stay in %APPDATA%\Star City Drift (same folder as before the rename).

NEW IN 2.1.1: WILLIAM BURKS
  - Matthew's oldest friend, William Burks, now turns up around Roanoke as the story moves on
    (the diner, downtown, the rail yards, and the Valley Gym after the ending).
  - Four new voiced side quests from William: Boys' Booth, High Score, Water the Flowers and
    Rematch. They are optional and don't count toward 100%.
  - Your saves carry over.

NEW IN 2.0.1: BLACKRIVER FIXES
  - Fixed the "two Silases" glitch: the cutscene copy of Silas (and the 2028 Matthew) no longer
    stays on the street after a Blackriver scene.
  - New Cinder Hollow guards: C.O.s, riot guards with shields, baton guards and a sergeant, in
    mixed squads. Tumbler Voss and the Lockkeeper have their own looks and their own lines.
  - New 2028 shouts and taunts for the guards, the Lockkeeper's crew, Voss and the Lockkeeper.

NEW IN 2.0.0: BLACKRIVER (FREE DLC)
  - A new story set in 2028. Matthew visits Silas Boone in Cinder Hollow and asks him about
    Blackriver. Then Silas breaks out in his orange jumpsuit, and by the end he joins Blackriver.
  - Nine new missions with cutscenes and voiced dialogue, two new bosses (Voss and the
    Lockkeeper), a new red suit, and the jumpsuit as an unlockable outfit. The ending leaves
    the door open for what comes next.
  - Start it from the "DLC: Blackriver" button on the title screen. It has its own save slot.
  Old saves carry over.

ALSO IN 1.9.0: CUTSCENES & VOICES
  - Real cutscenes: the big story moments now play as short scenes with black bars, camera moves
    and close-ups (the arrest on Mill Mountain, the prequel scenes and its closing scene). Press
    Esc, Start / B on a controller, or the Skip button on a phone to skip one.
  - Voices: the characters now speak their lines out loud, with talking portraits in the dialogue
    box. The voice volume follows the sound effects slider.
  - Camera zoom: press Q (R3 on a controller, or the zoom option in the pause menu) to switch
    between 1x, 2x and 3x. Close-up zoom keeps you in the middle and leans toward the fight.
  - Story: the free prequel is now set in spring 2015, with a teenage Matthew, a new last mission
    and a new ending. Marcus Hale has three new side missions at the river.
  Old saves carry over.

ALSO IN 1.8.0: HD REMASTER
  - Every character is redrawn at twice the detail: Matthew in all 23 outfits (including the Dark Star
    Suit and Cinder Hollow Orange), the thugs and bosses, Silas (prequel, boss and prison), the Cinder
    Hollow inmates and guards, Sgt. Reyes and Officer Okafor, and every street character.
  - The city is repainted at 2x too: all the street backgrounds, the six Cinder Hollow areas, the
    benches, trash cans and cars, with new inking, lighting and lettering.
  - The game now draws at 1920x1080 on a big screen (sharp on 1080p and high-DPI displays), with
    smooth edges on the new art. Phones and low-power PCs still draw at 960x540 to stay fast.
  - Nothing about how it plays has changed: same moves, same walk lanes, same saves. Weather, NG+
    and the prison all work as before.

NEW IN 1.7.0: LOCKED UP
  - NEW NAME: the game is now called Streets of Roanoke. Your saves, outfits and progress carry
    over. "Star City" is still what everyone in Roanoke calls the city.
  - NEW GAME+: after you beat the story, the title screen offers New Game+. The story starts over
    with every outfit, collectible and unlock kept, and enemies get a little tougher each cycle
    (the HUD shows NG+1, NG+2 and so on). Cinder Hollow and Cinder Hollow Orange stay unlocked.
  - WEATHER: pick Sunny, Night or Rainy on the title screen or in the pause menu, or press T
    (Create / View on a controller, the weather button on a phone). Sunny is bright daylight,
    Night has glowing streetlamps and neon, and Rainy brings the rain, wet streets and a soft
    rain sound. It only changes the look, and it is remembered on this computer.
  A new ending. When Silas Boone goes down on Mill Mountain, the patrol cars coming up the road
  belong to the honest police this time. Sgt. Dana Reyes and Officer Ben Okafor arrest him in the
  rain while Wren watches, cuff him and walk him down the mountain. Then comes the trial, "The
  People v. Silas Boone", and he is sent to Cinder Hollow Correctional Facility.
  - NEW AREA: CINDER HOLLOW. After you beat the story, take the Route 9 bus at the Community
    Center (a bus stop sign). Walk through the Visitor Lobby, Cell Block C (two tiers), the Mess
    Hall, the Library, the Workshop and the Yard. No fighting inside, just looking around.
  - Six prisoners with their own art and stories (some knew Wren, Boone's bookkeeper, Vera Lisk's
    courier, the Foreman's truck driver, one of the patrolmen Silas paid) and two guards.
  - Visit Silas in cell C-14. Pick what to ask him: why he did it, Wren, Vera, regrets. Find the
    old newspaper on the library return cart for one more question about how it all started.
  - NEW OUTFIT: CINDER HOLLOW ORANGE (outfit 15). A classic orange jumpsuit with the number 0417.
    It unlocks after your first visit to Silas's cell. Weapon: Mess Hall Tray. Signature move
    "Chow Line": a shoulder-first rush that knocks enemies over and shoves them back. Wear it with
    % (Shift+5), cycle with L1/R1 (LB/RB), or pick it in Pause > Outfits.
  - Old saves carry over. If your save already beat the story, Cinder Hollow is open when you load.
  Rated for teens.

ALSO IN 1.6.0: THE STAR GOES DARK
  The main story is rewritten as a darker mystery, "The Star Goes Dark".
  Matthew's friend Wren, the flower seller on Jefferson Street, disappears, and the police call
  her a runaway. Matthew finds out people have been vanishing for months, and every one of them
  owed money to Boone Holdings, Silas Boone's front company. Follow the trail through bought
  patrol cars in the Rail Yards, the Foreman's crew setting fires downtown and Vera Lisk's
  blackmail files, all the way to Mill Mountain on the city's anniversary night, when Silas
  blacks out the Star. Rescue Wren and turn the lights back on.
  - New opening card, story dialogue for every character, new mission briefs and a new ending.
  - Rainy night streets: falling rain, wet sidewalks, distant lightning, and a city blackout for
    the finale. The music is slower and moodier. Silas's theme is heavier.
  - The free prequel "Prequel: Silas Boone" now tells how Silas became that man, months earlier.
  - NEW OUTFIT: DARK STAR SUIT. Beat the story to unlock it (outfit 14). A black and midnight-blue
    suit with a cracked star on the chest that still glows faintly. Weapon: Dead Star Emblem.
    Signature move "Lights Out": a darkness pulse that dims the screen and staggers every enemy
    around you, in front and behind. Wear it with $ (Shift+4), cycle with L1/R1 (LB/RB), or pick
    it in Pause > Outfits. If your save already beat the story, it unlocks as soon as you load.
  Same missions, outfits, collectibles and difficulty as before. Old saves carry over. Rated for teens.

ALSO NEW IN 1.5.0: FREE PREQUEL DLC - "PREQUEL: SILAS BOONE"
  Play as Silas Boone, months before Matthew's story, on a night shift in the Roanoke districts.
  Six missions with new characters, two bosses (Vera Lisk and Mort "The Foreman" Kessler),
  five keepsakes to find, its own music and a closing scene that leads into the main story.
  HOW TO START IT: on the title screen choose "Prequel: Silas Boone (Free DLC)", then
  New (or Continue) and pick a difficulty. It's free and open from the start. It has its own
  save slot and never changes your main save. Pause > Save & Quit to Title takes you back.
  SILAS'S MOVES (keyboard / PlayStation / Xbox / touch):
    Gentleman's Jab   Z or J x3 / Cross / A / Z         3-hit combo, the 3rd hit pushes back
    Velvet Grip       C or L near a foe / Triangle / Y / GRIP   grab and throw (a strong hit on bosses)
    Iron Handshake    V / R2 / RT / Star                grab-slam with a shockwave that knocks down everyone nearby
    Last Word         X or K (hold to keep it up) / Circle / B / X   counter stance: take a hit for 0, answer with a knockdown
    Cold Stare        E with nobody to talk to / Square / X / E   thugs nearby hesitate (bosses don't)
    Boss Rush         sprint + punch, standing or moving (Shift+Z / L3 or L2 + Cross / LS or LT + A / RUN+Z)   shoulder charge through a line
  The full list is under Title > Controls.
  UNLOCK: beat the prequel to unlock SILAS'S SUIT for Matthew in the main game: outfit 13, weapon
  Iron Gauntlet, signature move Iron Handshake, and Matthew fights with all six of Silas's moves.
  Wear it with # (or Shift+3), cycle with L1/R1 (LB/RB), or pick it in Pause > Outfits. Old saves carry over.

UPDATES ARE AUTOMATIC
  When you're online, the app plays the latest version of the game from the Streets of Roanoke
  website. It only downloads the files that changed. Offline, it plays the copy that came with the
  installer. Your saves carry over either way. You don't need to reinstall to get new levels or
  fixes. If a new version comes out while you're playing, it loads the next time you're on the
  title screen.
