The Star Goes Dark

It's 2026, the night before Roanoke's anniversary, and it has rained for six straight days. Wren Calloway closes her flower cart on Jefferson Street and never makes it home. The police call her a runaway. Matthew Rose doesn't buy it.

Wren isn't the first. Everyone who has vanished owed money to Boone Holdings, and Silas Boone is buying the city one burned-out storefront at a time. Work your way through the Foreman's crew, a ring of blackmail files and the bought patrol cars in the Rail Yards to find out what Boone has planned for the Mill Mountain Star.

  • Free-roam side-scrolling across downtown, the Rail Yards, the river and Mill Mountain
  • Punches, kicks, grabs, combos and boss fights
  • Online co-op (new in 2.1.0): a friend joins as William Burks with an invite link or room code, on PC, phone or Chromebook
  • Side missions for the people who keep the neighborhood running
  • Sunny, Night and Rainy weather; New Game+; unlockable outfits

Characters

  • Matthew Rose

    The hero · 27

    Knows every block and most of the people on it. When his friend goes missing, he stops waiting for someone else to look.

  • Silas Boone

    Boone Holdings

    Lending, property and "protection". Polite, patient, and convinced the city already belongs to him.

  • Marcus Hale

    River Bridge tender

    Lights the bridge lamps and writes down everyone who crosses. Ask him for a hand, or offer one.

  • Wren Calloway

    City Market flower seller

    Matthew's friend. Her cart on Jefferson Street has been closed for days.

  • Dee Morales

    Dee's Diner

    Hears everything over the counter, and is the first to say Wren's gone quiet.

  • Priya Shah

    Valley Pages

    Keeps the list nobody else will: who's gone, and who they owed.

  • Hank "Rail" Pettigrew

    Rail Yards

    Has watched the wrong trucks roll past the wrong patrol cars for too many nights.

  • Cam Ortiz

    Rideshare driver

    Keeps getting sent to pins that don't make sense, by someone he can't say no to.

  • V

    Vera Lisk

    Keeper of files

    She doesn't need fists. She has something on everybody who might talk.

  • F

    The Foreman

    Mort Kessler · work-coat crew

    His crew carries matches, and shops that won't sell don't stay open long.

Also on the streets: June Whitaker, Coach Ray Delgado, Tessa Quinn and more.

Free · Prequel · 2015

Second Shift

Eleven years earlier, play as Silas Boone. Six missions follow him from protecting a single newsstand to running the city's crews, and to the night he signs Boone Holdings into being. A 16-year-old Matthew turns up in the cutscenes. The two never quite meet.

Added in 2.0.0 · Free · 2028

Blackriver

Two years after the Star came back on, Matthew visits Silas Boone in prison and asks one question: what is Blackriver? That night, Silas breaks out. Nine missions as an older Silas, from an orange C-14 jumpsuit to a new red suit, hunted by the masked Lockkeeper, all the way to a choice that sets up what comes next.
